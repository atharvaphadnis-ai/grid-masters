🎮 Gridmasters
Tic Tac Toe. Upgraded.

Gridmasters is a modern, fast-paced reimagination of the classic Tic-Tac-Toe game, designed with a sleek neon-themed interface and built for a smooth, mobile-first experience. It transforms a simple childhood game into a competitive and engaging platform with both real-time multiplayer and intelligent AI gameplay.

At its core, Gridmasters delivers two powerful modes. In Online Multiplayer, players can instantly create private rooms and share short, easy-to-use room codes with friends. Once connected, both players experience fully synchronized, real-time gameplay powered by modern web technologies. Every move appears instantly on both screens, ensuring a seamless and competitive experience. Strict turn enforcement guarantees fairness, while smart room handling prevents overcrowding and maintains a smooth session flow.

For solo players, the Offline Mode offers a challenging AI opponent powered by the Minimax algorithm. This ensures the bot makes optimal decisions, making it extremely difficult to defeat. Whether you’re practicing strategies or looking for a tough challenge, the AI provides a consistently competitive experience without requiring any internet connection.

The game features a clean and responsive 3x3 grid system designed specifically for touch interaction, making it perfect for smartphones. Every match is automatically evaluated for win conditions across rows, columns, and diagonals. At the end of each game, a clear result is displayed, announcing the winner or declaring a draw, followed by the option to continue playing.

Gridmasters also includes live score tracking, allowing players to keep track of wins, losses, and draws during their session. This adds a layer of competitiveness and encourages replayability.

Visually, the game stands out with its dark theme and neon accents, creating a futuristic gaming atmosphere. Smooth animations, glowing UI elements, and minimal design ensure that the interface remains both attractive and distraction-free.

Under the hood, Gridmasters is built using a robust full-stack architecture. The frontend is optimized for responsiveness and performance, while the backend ensures reliable real-time communication and session management. The system is designed to handle gameplay efficiently, with proper validation, error handling, and reconnection support for uninterrupted sessions.

Overall, Gridmasters is more than just Tic-Tac-Toe — it’s a polished, competitive, and modern gaming experience that combines simplicity with advanced features, making it perfect for both casual players and competitive users.
